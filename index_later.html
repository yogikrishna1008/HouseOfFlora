The workroom

House of Flora began with a single block-printed kurta made for a friend's sister, and a stubborn conviction that the good stuff had quietly disappeared from the market.

Today a small team cuts, prints and finishes everything in one workroom. Nothing is drop-shipped. Nothing is restocked once the cloth runs out. Every order is checked by a person before it is boxed, and if something is not right, you talk to that person, not a form.

Alterations up to two inches are included on every piece. Just say so when you order.
