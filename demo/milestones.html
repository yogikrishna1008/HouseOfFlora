04
Stage C · Future

Margin Protection

The stage where the store starts defending its own profit — not just taking orders.

Phase 2 of 2027

Once Stage B is running and you have a few months of real orders, this is where the website starts earning its keep differently — reducing returns, flagging risky COD orders before they ship, and converting returns to exchanges automatically. These are the problems Shopify apps charge ₹2,000–5,000 per month, each, to try to solve.

  • Per-garment size & fit guidance
  • Model-worn size on each product
  • COD risk scoring at checkout
  • Exchange-first returns flow
  • Return reason codes & stock re-entry
  • Repeat customer tagging
  • Shiprocket integration
  • Email order updates
